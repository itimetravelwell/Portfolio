Heart Shaped Room

Process of Modelling and Assembling a 3D Scene Enviroment

View Slide Deck

↑

The goal for this project was to create a unique space for mashups of tech that I love and also previous models I created learning how to use Maya. I choose to set the scene in an redesigned version of the Nakagin Hotel but instead a Hexagonal Prism to represent Toronto.

Reference Client:

Freelance

My Role:

Project Lead

Year:

2025

Service Provided:

3D Models, Substance Painter

I purposefully aimed high with this project, knowing it would be to showcase everything I had learned up until this point, I wanted to ensure the final render hit the lived in, but still having a futuristic aesthetic. Not only was I able to include previous 3D Models but also illustrations and designs, to really give the room a touch of my style and feeling.
